```
**Outcome** (1-year retrospective):

```

---

## Why This Format

- Thesis BEFORE outcome prevents hindsight bias
- Kill conditions force pre-commitment to exit triggers
- Confidence rating builds calibration over time
- Forensic check (v2.0) ensures no skipped due diligence
- v2.0 cap compliance prevents accidental rule violations
