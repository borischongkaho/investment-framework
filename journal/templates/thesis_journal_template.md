## Why This Format

- Forces written articulation of thesis (clarity test)
- Pre-commits to kill conditions (prevents goal-shifting)
- Tracks thesis evolution vs original (catches drift)
- Quarterly check-in prevents auto-pilot holding
- Exit triggers documented (no emotional exits)
